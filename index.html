Privacy Policy for SubBraker

Effective 28 August 2026 Last updated 28 August 2026

SubBraker helps you keep track of subscriptions you pay for elsewhere — what they cost, when they renew, and whether you still use them.

The short version

This policy explains what the app does with your information. SubBraker has no accounts, no analytics, and no servers of ours. Your subscription data stays on your device, and syncs only through your own iCloud account if you turn that on.

02

We do not have accounts, and we do not have your data

There is no sign-up and no login. We do not operate a server that stores your subscription information, and we have no ability to read what you record in the app.

We do not collect, sell, rent, or share personal information for advertising or any other purpose. SubBraker contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking technologies, and no third-party SDKs of any kind. The app does not track you across other apps or websites, and it does not use the Advertising Identifier.

03

What the app stores, and where

On your device

Everything you enter is stored locally on your device:

  • Subscription name, category, and optional notes
  • Price, currency, and billing cycle
  • Renewal, trial-end, and cancellation dates
  • Reminder settings
  • If you link an app for usage tracking: an opaque token supplied by Apple, plus the dates the app was last used

This data is held in the app’s private storage, protected by your device’s encryption and passcode.

In your own iCloud (Pro, optional)

If you have Pro and enable iCloud sync, your subscription records are mirrored to the private database of your own iCloud account so your devices stay in step.

This is your iCloud, not ours. The data is stored under your Apple Account, and we have no access to it, no ability to read it, and no copy of it. Apple’s handling of that data is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Sync is off unless you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time.

04

Receipt scanning happens on your device

If you scan a receipt or screenshot to fill in a subscription, the text recognition runs entirely on your device using Apple’s Vision framework.

The image is not uploaded anywhere. It is not sent to us, and it is not sent to any third party. We never see it. The image is used to read the text and is not retained by the app afterwards — only the details you choose to save are kept.

05

Screen Time and usage tracking (optional)

If you choose to link an app so SubBraker can tell you whether you still use a subscription, the app asks for Screen Time (Family Controls) permission. This is optional, and the rest of the app works without it.

When you grant it:

  • Apple gives the app an opaque token representing your chosen app. It is not a readable app name and cannot be used to identify you.
  • A small extension is woken by the system when that app is used, and writes a single timestamp to storage shared between the extension and the app.
  • Nothing about your usage is transmitted off the device. We receive none of it.

Screen Time data is treated as sensitive by Apple and by us. It is never sold, shared, or used for advertising. You can revoke the permission at any time in iOS Settings, or unlink the app inside SubBraker.

06

The one network request the app makes

To convert subscription costs into a single currency, SubBraker fetches daily exchange rates from Frankfurter (api.frankfurter.dev), an open exchange-rate service.

Being straightforward about this:

  • The request contains currency codes only — for example “USD” and “EUR”. It contains no subscription names, no prices, no device identifiers, and nothing personal.
  • As with any request over the internet, the service necessarily sees the IP address the request comes from. We do not control or receive that.
  • Rates are cached on your device so the app works offline.

This is the only network request SubBraker makes for its own functionality.

07

Purchases

Pro is sold as a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime unlock, and is purchased through Apple’s In-App Purchase system.

Apple processes the payment, not us. We never see your card number, billing address, or Apple Account credentials. We receive only the confirmation that a purchase or renewal is valid, which is what unlocks Pro features.

Purchases, refunds, and subscription management are handled by Apple and governed by Apple’s terms and privacy policy. You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in your Apple Account settings.

08

Notifications

SubBraker can remind you before a subscription renews. These are local notifications, scheduled and delivered on your device. The content does not pass through us or through any third party.

If you enable iCloud sync, your device also receives silent notifications from Apple’s servers telling the app that data changed on another of your devices. These carry no content of their own.

09

Children

SubBraker is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from children. Since the app collects no personal information at all, there is no children’s data for us to hold.

10

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is generally nothing for us to retrieve, correct, or delete on your behalf. You remain in full control:

  • Access and export: your data is visible in the app on your device.
  • Correct: edit or delete any entry directly in the app.
  • Delete everything: deleting the app removes its local data. If you used iCloud sync, you can also remove the app’s data from your Apple Account in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.
  • Withdraw permissions: revoke Screen Time access or notifications at any time in iOS Settings.

If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, the GDPR gives you rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection, and the right to complain to your local supervisory authority. If you are in California, the CCPA gives you rights to know, delete, and opt out of sale — we do not sell personal information, and never have.

To exercise any right, or if you believe we hold something about you, write to us at adp@misakanetwork.ca.

11

Data retention

We retain nothing, because we receive nothing. Data you enter is kept on your device, and in your own iCloud if you enable sync, for as long as you choose to keep it.
